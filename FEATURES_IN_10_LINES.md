# What the App Does (10 simple lines)

1. **Digital Arrest Shield:** listens to a call and raises a red alert when it hears scam phrases like "I am from the CBI, don't tell anyone, send the money".
2. **SMS check:** catches fake bank or KYC messages and explains what is wrong with the message or link.
3. **Number check:** shows whether an unknown number is foreign or has been reported as a scam by someone.
4. **QR / UPI check:** explains that scanning a QR code sends money out; it never brings money in.
5. **Deepfake video call check:** reads the pulse in the face to tell whether the person on screen is real or a fake video.
6. **Fake document check:** checks whether an ID image has been edited.
7. **Alert:** when something looks like a scam, a warning appears on screen and as a phone notification, along with the reasons why it looks fake.
8. **AI explanation:** at the tap of a button, AI explains in simple English why a message looks like a scam.
9. **Help & Report:** one tap to call 1930, open cybercrime.gov.in, alert family, and create an evidence report for the police.
10. **Dashboard and feedback:** shows how many scams were caught, and the app keeps learning from 👍 👎 feedback.

**Limits (for the viva):** no app stops 100% of scams. Automatic checking of calls and SMS needs a native Android app (not built yet).
The app cannot trace the scammer's location; that is the job of the telecom company and the police. Integration with banks, telecom operators and I4C is future scope.
