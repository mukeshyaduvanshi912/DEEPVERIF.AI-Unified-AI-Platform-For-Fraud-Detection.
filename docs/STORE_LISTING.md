# Play Store listing draft

**App name:** DeepVerif.AI — Scam Shield
**Short description (80 chars):** Spot digital-arrest calls, fake IDs, deepfake faces and phishing SMS.
**Full description:**
DeepVerif.AI helps you stop common Indian online frauds before money is lost.
- Digital Arrest Shield: listens to a call you choose to monitor and warns when the caller uses the scam script (fake police/CBI, "don't tell anyone", "transfer to safe account") — even if the caller is polite.
- Payment Guard: pauses risky transfers made during a suspicious call.
- Deepfake check: a 6-second camera scan looks for a real pulse signal in the face.
- Fake ID check: finds edited areas in Aadhaar/PAN-style images or PDFs.
- SMS Phishing check: share any suspicious message to the app.
- Data Exposure tips: how scammers get your details and how to lock them down.
- Tamper-evident log you can show to the cyber-crime helpline (1930).
This is an assistive tool, not a guarantee. Report fraud at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.
