# DeepVerif.AI — Privacy Policy
Last updated: 2 October 2026 · Contact: <replace with team e-mail>

DeepVerif.AI is an app that helps detect online fraud.

**What we process**
- Camera frames (liveness scan) — analysed in memory and not stored.
- Speech converted to text by your browser/device in the Shield tab — the text is analysed; **only the verdict, score and matched categories are logged, never the conversation.**
- Uploaded ID images/PDFs and pasted SMS text — analysed to give a result; files are not stored. A short text preview of an SMS may appear in the audit log.
- Links inside an SMS may be opened by our server to read their redirect chain and domain age.
- Password check: hashed in your browser; only the first 5 characters of the hash go to the Have I Been Pwned range API. We never receive your password.
- E-mail breach check (optional): the address is sent to Have I Been Pwned if enabled; we log only the outcome.

**What we do not do:** sell data, show ads, read your SMS inbox, record calls, or access contacts.
**Audit log:** a hash-chained list of detection verdicts kept on the server for tamper-evidence.
**Your choices:** do not upload real ID documents (use dummy data); contact us to delete log entries linked to you.
**Children:** not intended for users under 18.
**Limits:** results are assistive and can be wrong. For fraud, call 1930 or visit cybercrime.gov.in.
