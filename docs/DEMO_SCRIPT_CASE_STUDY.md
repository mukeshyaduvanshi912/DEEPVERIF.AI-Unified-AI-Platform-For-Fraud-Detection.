# Demo script — "Senior citizen digitally arrested for 4 days, Rs.52 lakh lost"

Source: Dainik Jagran newspaper clipping (photo from the team). Facts used from the article: video call, caller claimed to be an ATS (Pune) officer on 28 Sept,
accused of terror links + money laundering, "ATS and NIA investigation", fake Supreme Court arrest warrant, victim broke 3 FDs and sent money over 4 days, got suspicious and complained to the cyber-crime police station.
The call text in the demo is a **re-enactment written by us from these facts, not a real recording** — say this out loud.

**Total time: 4-5 minutes. Speaker roles: A = story + close, B = Shield + liveness, C = proof (SMS/doc/log/tests).**

| Time | Who | What you do | What you say (short) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | A | Show the news clipping on slide / phone | "This is a real incident: a 65-year-old was kept under 'digital arrest' on a video call for 4 days and lost Rs.52 lakh. Our project is built to stop cases like this." |
| 0:30 | A | Show timeline slide: Day 1 call -> accusation -> warrant -> FD break -> transfers -> suspicion | "There were 5 points in this case where it could have been stopped." |
| 1:00 | B | Shield tab -> tap **Digital arrest case study (Hindi)** | "The caller mentioned ATS, NIA, a Supreme Court warrant, terrorism and money laundering. Our Shield catches this script and shows HIGH risk, with a red banner and popup, in the very first minute." |
| 1:45 | B | (Optional) Start Listening, read 2 lines aloud in Hindi | "This works with the live mic too." |
| 2:00 | B | Scroll to Payment guard (values already filled: Rs.17 lakh, new payee, 120 min call) -> Check this transfer | "The victim broke his FDs and sent the money. Here the transfer would be PAUSED, because of the new payee + large amount + ongoing call." |
| 2:30 | B | Family alert -> enter a teammate's number -> Create alert | "Scams depend on isolation. This instantly sends a WhatsApp alert to family." |

| 4:40 | A | Close: Data Exposure tab / 1930 | "The victim complained only after he became suspicious. We want that suspicion to come on day one. If you are defrauded, call 1930." |

## Do's and don'ts
- Say "re-enactment based on the news report"; never claim it is the real call transcript.
- Don't promise we would have stopped this case: say "we would have raised alerts at several points".
- Keep the clipping photo; cite newspaper + city + date in slide footer (read the date off the clipping).
- Backup: if mic or network fails, the sample buttons work without internet.
