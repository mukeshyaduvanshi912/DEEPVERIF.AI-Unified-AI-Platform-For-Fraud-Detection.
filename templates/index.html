      🧠 How to recognise common scams

      Digital Arrest

      "I am from the CBI/police, you are under digital arrest, don't tell anyone, don't cut the call." A real agency never arrests you or asks for money on a video call. Hang up and call 1930.

      Bank / KYC

      "Your account will be blocked, update KYC now" + a link. Banks do not do KYC through SMS links. Verify only through the bank's official app or branch.

      Courier / Parcel

      "Drugs were found in your parcel." First check yourself through the courier's official site/number. Do not send money out of fear.

      UPI / Refund

      "Enter your PIN for a refund, or approve a collect request." You never need to enter a UPI PIN to receive money.

      Job / Investment / Task

      "Earn from home, pay a small deposit first." If they ask for money first, it is a scam.

      Tamper-Evident Audit Log

      Every detection event is written to a SHA-256 hash chain. If any past record is altered, chain verification will fail — useful as evidence for reporting to authorities.
